1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Ohr AI, Inc. (“Ohr AI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a Delaware corporation, collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with the Ohr AI mobile application, our website, and other interactions you have with us, including when you contact us for support or through social media (together, the “Services”). It also describes the choices and rights you have regarding your personal information.

  • Personal Information. In this Privacy Policy, “personal information” means information that identifies you, relates to you, or can reasonably be linked to you.
  • Availability. The Services are currently available in the United States and Taiwan. Additional information for residents of certain jurisdictions is set out in Section 12.
  • Eligibility. The Services are intended for individuals 18 years of age and older, as described in Section 9.
  • Third-Party Services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to the services of third parties, such as Apple, Google, or social media platforms, even when you use them in connection with the Services. Those third parties’ own privacy policies govern how they handle your information.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information as described below.

Information You Provide. When you create an account and use the Services, you provide:

  • Account Information: Your email address, which we receive from Google or Apple when you sign in with your Google or Apple account, and, if you sign in with Google, your name and a link to your Google profile photo. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email address you choose to share, which may be a relay address Apple generates for you. If you continue as a guest, we create an account for you without an email address;
  • Profile and Intake Information: Information about your career, such as your current role or situation, target roles, job descriptions you share with us, work history, skills, education, certifications, the time you can commit each week, and career goals; your first and last name; your year of birth and, if you choose to provide it, your gender; and, if you choose to provide them, your current pay, whether you are currently employed, and the city where you want to work;
  • Documents and Evidence: Resumes, files, photos, links, and written responses you submit during sign-up or onboarding or to complete tasks, including any information stored inside them;
  • Tasks You Propose: Tasks you suggest through the Services, and any information you include with them; and
  • Communications: Information you provide when you contact us by email or through social media, including the content of your messages.

Information We Create About You. When you use the Services, we create information about you, including:

  • Scores: Your career scores and their ceilings;
  • Estimates: Salary estimates we generate for you, and how the pay you provide compares with them;
  • Verification Results: The results of verifying the evidence you submit, and the written reasons for your scores and results;
  • Task Information: The missions and tasks assigned to you or that you propose, our assessment of tasks you propose, your progress, the time you take to complete tasks, your attempts, and the results;
  • Extracted Features: The text of resumes and job descriptions you upload; features identified in the information and evidence you provide, such as skills and experience; and, for resume and job description files you upload, an identifier derived from the file; and
  • Records: Records of the versions of the instructions and AI models used to produce your scores, verification results, and salary estimates.

Information Collected Automatically. When you use the Services, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Your device model, operating system and version, and app version;
  • Usage Information: How you use features of the app, and the dates and times of your activity;
  • Log Information: Your IP address and information about requests your device makes to our systems; and
  • Diagnostic Information: Crash reports, including those we receive from Apple, and performance information.

If You Join Our Beta. If you participate in our beta through Apple TestFlight, we receive from Apple the name and email address used to invite you, and any feedback you send through TestFlight, including screenshots and comments. We use this information to manage the beta and contact you about it. This information is separate from your Ohr AI account.

Information We Do Not Request. We do not ask for, and our tasks are designed not to require, bank or other financial account records; government-issued identification documents; Social Security or other national identification numbers; usernames or passwords for other online accounts; residential addresses; criminal records; or health records. You are responsible for removing this information from documents before you submit them.

Sensitive Information You Include Without Our Request. Documents and evidence you submit may contain information we do not request, including sensitive information such as information about religion, political views, union membership, race or ethnicity, or health. Our scoring is designed to evaluate your skills, experience, education, and the evidence of your work. Because we process each document as a whole, sensitive information in a document is stored with it and sent to our AI model provider with the rest of the document. You are responsible for removing this information from documents before you submit them. Documents may also contain information about other people, such as references, colleagues, or the recipients of messages. You are responsible for submitting only information you are entitled to share and for removing other people’s details before you submit a document. We process all such information only as part of the document you submitted.

Photo and Facial Information. When you take or choose a photo to complete a task, the app removes its location and camera metadata before uploading it. Photos you upload as your resume, and PDFs you upload, may retain information stored inside the file, such as the date and location a photo was taken. We do not use facial recognition or analyze facial features in photos you submit, and we do not use photos to identify you. We use photos to read the documents they show, to assess whether they plausibly show the work, event, or credential claimed for a task, and, during the beta period, to improve verification as described in Section 5. We do not instruct our AI models to identify or assess the people in a photo. We do not collect biometric identifiers, such as face geometry, from any photo.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information for the following purposes.

To Provide the Services. We use personal information to:

  • Create and maintain your account;
  • Analyze the information and documents you provide during sign-up or onboarding and in completing tasks;
  • Calculate your career scores, their ceilings, and salary estimates;
  • Generate missions and tasks suited to your profile, goals, and progress, including your scores and how quickly and how well you complete tasks;
  • Review tasks you propose to determine whether they align with your career goals and can be added to your plan;
  • Verify the evidence you submit to complete tasks, including by assessing whether it plausibly shows the claimed work, event, or credential, and matches the task’s requirements;
  • Generate written reasons explaining your scores and verification results; and
  • Provide your task history in the app.

How Your Scores Are Produced. Your scores are produced through automated processing that uses AI models to analyze the information and evidence you provide. We keep a record of the version of the scoring instructions and the AI model used to produce each score and verification result. Your scores are estimates, as described in Section 6. Within the Services, we use your scores and how you complete tasks to determine which tasks you receive next. We do not use your scores to make decisions about you outside the Services.

To Improve the Services. We use personal information to understand how the Services are used, to diagnose and fix problems, and to develop and improve features. During the beta period, we also review the evidence you submit to improve the accuracy of verification, as described in Section 5.

To Present the Services. We may use information about how you use the Services, with identifying details removed, to illustrate the Services to prospective investors and business partners.

To Produce Aggregated or De-identified Information. We may create aggregated or de-identified information that can no longer reasonably be linked to you, and may use and disclose it for any lawful purpose, including to analyze and improve the Services, to produce and publish statistics about how the Services are used, and for research and business purposes. We handle such information as described in Sections 4 and 5.

To Communicate With You. We use personal information to send you service communications, such as security alerts and notices of changes to this Privacy Policy; to respond to messages you send us, including through social media; and to manage our beta program and contact you about it.

For Safety, Security, and Integrity. We use personal information to protect the Services and our users, including to:

  • Secure your account and our systems;
  • Detect and prevent fraud and misuse;
  • Review content submitted to the Services to identify and address content that violates our rules for using the Services or the law;
  • Review tasks you propose to identify activities that may be unsafe, unlawful, or harmful;
  • Review reports that someone under 18 years of age is using the Services, as described in Section 9;
  • Protect the rights, property, and safety of our users, Ohr AI, and others; and
  • Enforce our rules for using the Services.

For Legal Compliance. We use personal information to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests from public authorities, handle requests to exercise your rights, establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, and maintain records required by law.

Use of AI Models. We use third-party AI models to provide the Services, as described in Section 4.

New Purposes. If we wish to use personal information for a purpose that is materially different from those described in this Privacy Policy, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required by law or where the new purpose would apply to information we already hold, ask for your consent, as described in Section 11.

4. How We Disclose Personal Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or advertising purposes. We disclose personal information only as described in this section.

Service Providers. We disclose personal information to service providers that perform services on our behalf. They access, process, and store personal information only as needed to perform those services, in accordance with our instructions and their contractual obligations to us. They include providers of:

  • AI model processing: Anthropic, PBC processes the information and documents you provide on our behalf to provide the Services, including to read documents and photos, calculate scores, estimate salaries from the pay, employment status, and city you provide, generate missions and tasks, and review the tasks you propose and the evidence you submit;
  • Cloud hosting and databases;
  • File storage;
  • Network delivery and security services;
  • Email and communication services;
  • Account sign-in, provided by Google and Apple; and
  • App distribution and crash reporting, provided by Apple.

Employers and Recruiters. We do not disclose your scores, verification results, or profile to employers or recruiters. If you share your resume through your resume link, the people you share it with, including employers and recruiters, can view and download your resume, as described under “Resume Sharing” below.

Legal Requirements and Protection of Rights. We may disclose personal information to government authorities, courts, or other third parties where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law, a legal process, or a lawful request from a public authority; to enforce our rules for using the Services and investigate potential violations; to detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues; to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims; to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, Ohr AI, or the public; or in an emergency involving a risk of death or serious physical injury to a person.

Professional Advisors. We may disclose personal information to our lawyers, accountants, auditors, and insurers, where necessary for them to provide their services to us, subject to confidentiality obligations.

Prospective Investors and Partners. We may disclose information about how you use the Services, with identifying details removed, to prospective investors and business partners.

Business Transfers. If Ohr AI is involved in, or negotiating, a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, receivership, sale of all or part of its assets, or transition of the Services to another provider, personal information may be disclosed to the parties involved and their advisors, subject to confidentiality obligations, including during due diligence, whether or not the transaction is completed, and may be transferred as part of the transaction.

Aggregated or De-identified Information. We may disclose information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it can no longer reasonably be linked to you, including by publishing it. We handle such information as described in Section 5.

Resume Sharing. When you open the Resume page in the app, we create a QR code and link for the resume file you uploaded. Anyone who scans the code or has the link can view and download your resume without signing in, including employers or recruiters you show it to and anyone they forward it to. The link opens your current resume, including a resume you upload later to replace it. It does not include your scores, verification results, or other profile information. To turn off the link, delete your resume on the Resume page or delete your account. If you upload a resume again, we create a new link. People who have already downloaded your resume keep their copy, and their use of it is not governed by this Privacy Policy.

At Your Direction. We disclose personal information when you direct us to do so. Information you share with third parties is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as we need it to provide the Services, or for other legitimate purposes such as complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and maintaining the security of the Services. How long we retain personal information depends on the type of information and how we use it.

Information We Retain Until You Delete It. We retain the following information for as long as you have an account, unless you delete it sooner:

  • Your account and profile information;
  • Your scores, salary estimates, verification results, and the written reasons for them;
  • Your task history; and
  • The text and features we extract from documents and evidence you submit, the names of files you submit, and, for resume and job description files, an identifier derived from the file.

When your account is deleted, we delete or de-identify this information within the period required by applicable law and, in any case, without undue delay, unless we retain it for longer as described below.

Evidence You Submit During the Beta. During the beta period, we retain the original files, photos, and documents you submit so that we can review them to improve verification and the Services. Members of our team may review your submissions for this purpose. We retain these originals until you delete your account. If we begin deleting them sooner, for example when the beta period ends, we will update this Privacy Policy. We keep the verification result, the written reasons, the features extracted from your submissions, and the names of the files, as described above. If you delete your account, these originals are deleted with it, as described in Section 6.

Other Information. We retain other information, such as device, usage, and crash information and messages you send us, only for as long as needed for the purposes for which we collected it.

Information We Retain for Longer for Legal, Security, or Safety Reasons. In some cases, we retain personal information for longer, even after you delete it or your account, where this is necessary:

  • To comply with a legal obligation, such as a lawful request or order from a public authority, for the duration of that obligation;
  • To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims;
  • To investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our rules for using the Services; and
  • To demonstrate that we have handled your requests as required by law, for example by retaining a record of a request to delete your information, without retaining the information that was deleted.

De-identified Information. We may retain information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it can no longer reasonably be linked to you. We maintain such information in de-identified form and do not attempt to re-identify it, except as required by law.

Backups. Deleted information may remain in our backup systems for a limited period before it is overwritten.

Service Providers. Our service providers may retain personal information they process on our behalf for limited periods under their own terms.

How We Determine Retention Periods. In setting retention periods, we consider the purpose for which we process the information; the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the information; the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure; whether we can achieve our purposes by other means; and applicable legal requirements.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have choices about the personal information we hold about you. This section describes the rights available to all users and how to exercise them. Residents of certain jurisdictions have additional rights, described in Section 12.

Controls in the App. You can delete your account in the app’s settings. If you use a guest account, signing out of it also deletes it. On the Resume page, you can delete your resume, which also turns off your resume link. The text and other information we extracted from your resume are retained until you delete your account.

Account Deletion. You can delete your account at any time in the app’s settings. If you are unable to do so, you can ask us to delete it as described below. When your account is deleted, we delete or de-identify your personal information within the period required by applicable law and, in any case, without undue delay, except as described in Section 5 or where we are required by law to retain it. Deleting the app from your device does not delete your account. If you signed in with Apple, you can also stop using Sign in with Apple with Ohr AI in your Apple Account settings.

Guest Accounts. A guest account does not have an email address and can be used only on the device where you created it. Signing out of a guest account deletes it. If you later sign in with Apple or Google, you start a new account, and we delete your guest account; its information is not transferred. Deleting the app from your device does not delete a guest account.

Deleting Submissions. Documents and evidence you have submitted, including your resume, are deleted from our systems when you delete your account, and your resume link stops working, except as described in Section 5. Copies that other people downloaded through your resume link, or that you saved outside the app, are not affected.

Access. You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Correction. You can ask us to correct personal information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

About Your Scores. Your scores and verification results are produced through automated processing, as described in Section 3. The written reasons and other information generated by AI models may contain errors, and your scores are estimates, not definitive assessments of your abilities or prospects. Your scores may change over time as the information you provide, our models, and our rules change.

Withdrawing Consent. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it. If you withdraw consent that is needed for a feature, you may be unable to use that feature.

Communications. We send service communications, such as security alerts and notices of changes to this Privacy Policy, while you have an account.

How to Make a Request. Except for actions you can take in the app, send requests to exercise the rights described in this section to privacy@ohr-ai.com. In your request, tell us which right you want to exercise. For questions about using the Services, including how your scores work, contact support@ohr-ai.com.

Verification. To protect your information, we verify your identity before acting on a request, normally by confirming the request through the email address associated with your account. If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email address, this is the relay address Apple generated for you. Guest accounts do not have an email address, so we may be unable to verify requests about them; you can delete a guest account at any time in the app. If we cannot verify your identity, we will not be able to act on your request. We do not act on account or privacy requests made through social media, as described in Section 10.

Authorized Agents. Where permitted by law, you may submit a request through an authorized agent. We will ask the agent for proof that you authorized them, and we may ask you to confirm your identity directly.

Response Time. We respond to requests within the periods required by applicable law. If we need more time, we will tell you why and when to expect a response, within any extension the law allows.

Limits on Requests. We may decline or limit a request where the law permits or requires it, for example where fulfilling it would reveal another person’s personal information or our confidential business information, where we must retain information to comply with a legal obligation, or where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive. If we decline a request, we will explain why.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure. These safeguards are designed to be appropriate to the nature of the information we process, including the documents and evidence you submit.

Encryption. Personal information is encrypted in transit between your device and our systems using industry-standard transport encryption.

Storage and Access Controls. Files you submit are stored in private storage that is not publicly accessible, except your resume when you share it through your resume link, as described in Section 4. Access to personal information by our personnel is limited to those who need it to operate, support, and secure the Services.

Data Minimization. We limit the personal information we hold to reduce the impact of any security incident. We do not request the categories of information listed in Section 2 under “Information We Do Not Request.” When you take or choose a photo to complete a task, the app removes its location and camera metadata before uploading it. We retain the original files you submit only as described in Section 5.

Information on Your Device. The app keeps a copy of your resume on your device, which you can also open in the Files app, along with some of the information you provide during sign-up, such as your name, and the information that keeps you signed in. This information is protected by your device’s built-in data protection and may be included in backups of your device. Signing out or deleting your account removes the copy of your resume from the app, and deleting your account also removes the information you provided during sign-up. Deleting the app removes its files from your device, but your device may keep the information that keeps you signed in. Photos and files you attach to tasks are not kept in the app on your device.

Service Providers. We require service providers that process personal information on our behalf to protect it under contract, and we select providers that maintain security practices appropriate to the information they process.

Monitoring and Review. We update our safeguards as the Services and potential risks change.

Your Role in Security. You can help protect your information by keeping your device secure, using a passcode or biometric lock on your device, and not sharing access to your account. You are responsible for deciding what information to include in documents you submit and for covering details a task does not require before uploading, including information stored inside a file, such as the location where a photo was taken. If you believe your account has been accessed without authorization, contact us immediately at privacy@ohr-ai.com.

Security Incidents. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

Reporting a Vulnerability. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in the Services, please report it to support@ohr-ai.com.

8. International Transfers

Ohr AI, Inc. is based in the United States. Wherever you use the Services, including from the United States or Taiwan, your personal information is stored in Japan, where our cloud hosting provider operates the data center we use, and is processed in the United States, where our AI model provider operates.

Service Providers. Our service providers, described in Section 4, process personal information on our behalf in the United States, Japan, and other countries where they operate.

Access by Our Personnel. Members of our team located in the United States and Taiwan may access personal information where necessary to operate, support, and secure the Services, subject to the access controls described in Section 7.

Safeguards. Data protection laws in the United States and Japan may differ from those in the country where you live. We apply the protections described in this Privacy Policy to your personal information regardless of where it is processed. Additional information for residents of Taiwan is set out in Section 12.

Access by Public Authorities. Personal information processed in another country may be subject to the laws of that country, including laws that permit access by courts, law enforcement, and other public authorities.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Services are intended for individuals 18 years of age and older. If you are under 18 years of age, do not use the Services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe someone under 18 is using the Services, you can report it to privacy@ohr-ai.com. Reports are handled as described in Section 6, and we do not act on reports made through social media messages. We review each report and may ask the account holder to confirm their age. While we review a report, we may limit some features on the account. If we determine that an account belongs to someone under 18 years of age, we will delete the account.

10. Social Media

Ohr AI has accounts on Instagram and other social media platforms. When you interact with Ohr AI there, for example by following us, commenting, tagging us, or sending us a message, the platform collects information under its own privacy policy. We receive your username, public profile information, and the content you send us, along with aggregate statistics about our audience. We use this to respond to you and to understand who follows Ohr AI, and we handle anything you send us under this Privacy Policy. Direct messages are not a secure channel for documents; use the app or contact support@ohr-ai.com instead. We do not share the content of your direct messages publicly. We may feature content you post about Ohr AI with your permission, or by resharing posts in which you tag us using the platform’s built-in sharing features.

Links to Ohr AI. Links in our social media posts may include tags that tell us which post brought you to Ohr AI.

Public Comments. Comments you post on our social media pages are public. We do not discuss individual accounts in public replies. We may hide or remove comments that are spam, abusive, or share someone else’s personal information.

Account and Privacy Requests. We cannot verify your identity through social media messages, so we do not make changes to your account through them. To delete your account, obtain a copy of your information, or make any other privacy request, use the app or contact privacy@ohr-ai.com as described in Section 6. For questions about using the Services, contact support@ohr-ai.com.

Protecting Yourself from Impersonation. We do not ask for your password, payment information, or identity documents in social media messages. If an account claiming to be Ohr AI’s official account or to represent us asks for these, it is not us; please report it to privacy@ohr-ai.com and to the platform.

Requests may be submitted as described in Section 6.

Specific Purposes and Categories of Personal Information. The categories of personal information we collect include: (C001) information identifying an individual; (C011) personal descriptions; (C038) occupation; (C051) school records; (C052) qualifications or skills; (C056) works; (C061) current employment; (C062) employment history; (C064) work experience; (C068) salary and withholdings; (C071) evaluation of work performance and potential; (C072) training records; and (C132) unclassified information.
